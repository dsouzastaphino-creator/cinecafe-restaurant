Our Origin

Our Story

Born from a love of great cinema and exceptional coffee — CineCafe is where two worlds collide into one unforgettable experience.

The Beginning

Why We Built This

We realized that modern multiplexes had lost their soul — sterile halls with overpriced popcorn, no ambiance, no intimacy. Meanwhile, the coffee shops we loved offered beautiful spaces but nothing to do in them beyond scroll our phones.

CineCafe was our answer. A sanctuary where you arrive early for an exceptional cup of coffee, eat a proper meal before the film, and then step into a 35-seat dome theatre where every chair feels like the director intended you to sit in it.

This isn't a cinema. This isn't a restaurant. It's both — simultaneously, intentionally, and joyfully.
