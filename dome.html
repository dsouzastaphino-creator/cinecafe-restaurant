The Architecture

The Dome is an acoustically treated marvel designed to provide the ultimate immersive auditory and visual experience. With seating strictly limited to 35 guests per screening, every seat is optimized.

Designed by acclaimed architects, the structure relies on a minimalist geometric frame paired with state-of-the-art projection technology. The curvature of the walls eliminates standing sound waves, resulting in crystalline audio fidelity.
